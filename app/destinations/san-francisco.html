Destination San Francisco

San Francisco, California is home to the Arize office, now part of Dynatrace, located at 260 California St, 4th Floor, San Francisco, CA 94111 — in the Financial District, the city's downtown business core. San Francisco is the heart of Northern California's tech scene, and the Financial District is where the city's towers, plazas and historic streets sit side by side.

For a meal and a drink, Schroeder's at 240 Front Street is a historic German beer hall, founded in 1893 and inspired by Bavaria's beer halls: cavernous, high-energy and often rowdy with a corporate crowd. It is a lively contrast to a quiet day of observability work.

Visiting the Arize San Francisco office means exploring the Financial District on foot. Look up: dozens of secret rooftop gardens and sunlit indoor plazas known as POPOS (privately owned public open spaces) are legally open to the public for free inside and on top of major office towers, a legacy of the city's 1985 Downtown Plan. Embarcadero BART and Muni Metro station and the California Street cable car serve the area. San Francisco rewards the curious, and so does the team here.
